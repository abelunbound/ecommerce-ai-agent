# Returns and Refunds Policy

> Archived version. This policy was replaced by Returns and Refunds Policy v2.0 on 1 September 2026 and applies only to orders placed before that date.

## Return window

You can return most items within 28 days of the date of delivery (the delivery day counts as day 1).

Items marked "Sale" or "Final Sale" cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Free UK returns

All UK returns, including change-of-mind returns, use our free prepaid label. No deduction is made from your refund.

## Exchanges

You can make one free exchange for a different size or colour, within the 28-day window and subject to stock.

## Condition and special items

- Items should be unused or lightly tested and complete with accessories.
- In-ear earbuds and ear tips cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless faulty.
- Unused power banks and loose lithium batteries must use the ground-only label.
- Engraved or custom-configured items cannot be returned unless faulty.

## Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 working days of us receiving your return. The standard delivery charge is refunded when the whole order is returned.

## International orders

For change-of-mind returns from outside the UK you pay the return shipping.
