# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Overview

This policy applies to orders placed on or after 1 September 2026. Orders placed before that date are handled under the version in force on the order date (see the [archived policy](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/archive/returns-2025-01)).

If an item is faulty, the faulty-goods route in [Warranty and Faulty Goods](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/warranty) applies instead and is more generous than the change-of-mind rules below.

## Your return window

- **Standard items:** 30 days for change of mind, counted from the day after delivery (the delivery date on carrier tracking), not the order date. These are calendar days. An item delivered on 1 October can be registered for return up to and including 31 October.
- **Sale items** (marked "Sale" on the product page): 14 days from the day after delivery.
- **Final Sale items** (marked "Final Sale" at checkout): cannot be returned for change of mind. Faulty Final Sale items are still covered by your statutory rights.
- **Orders delivered in several parcels:** the window runs from the day after the last parcel arrives.

This voluntary window goes beyond the legal minimum cancellation period of 14 days under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. It does not affect your statutory rights.

## Condition of returned items

Items should be unused or only lightly tested, complete with accessories, manuals and, where possible, original packaging. Please remove personal data and sign out of any accounts on devices before returning them.

Where an item comes back in a condition materially worse than when we sent it, we may reduce the refund by a reasonable amount.

## Items with special rules

### Hygiene items

In-ear earbuds and replacement ear tips cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless they are faulty. Over-ear and on-ear headphones can be returned if the ear pads are clean and undamaged. Sealed software and licence keys cannot be returned once activated or redeemed.

### Batteries and power banks

Unused power banks and other loose lithium batteries can be returned within the window, but only using our ground-only returns label (a QR code for drop-off at a ground-service location). They cannot be sent by any air service. If a battery is swollen, hot or damaged, do not ship it; follow the safety steps in [Contact and Escalation](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/contact-and-escalation).

### Personalised and made-to-order items

Engraved or custom-configured items cannot be returned for change of mind. They can be returned if faulty.

### High-value items (over £500)

Items over £500 need a return authorisation before sending. We supply a prepaid, tracked and insured label, which you must use. Please keep the original packaging; we cannot accept responsibility for damage caused by inadequate packaging.

### Bundles and free gifts

Returning part of a bundle can change the bundle price, and a free gift or threshold discount may be recalculated if your order falls below the qualifying spend. See [Promotions and Gift Cards](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/promotions-and-gift-cards).

## How to return an item

1. Register the return in your account under Orders, then Return item, within your return window.
2. Choose a label (or QR code) and wait for the confirmation email.
3. Pack the item securely and send it within 14 days of registering the return.
4. Track the parcel until it reaches our returns centre.

Returns that are not registered may be delayed or refused.

## Return shipping costs

### UK orders

- **Change of mind using our prepaid label:** £3.99 is deducted from your refund.
- **Arranging your own return:** at your own cost. We recommend a tracked service, as you are responsible for the parcel until it reaches us.
- **Faulty, damaged or wrong item:** free. We pay the return shipping.

### International orders

For change-of-mind returns from outside the UK you pay the return shipping, whichever route you use. See [International Shipping](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/shipping-international) for duties and taxes.

## Refunds

- **Timing:** within 5 working days of us receiving and inspecting the return. Inspection normally takes 1 to 3 working days after the parcel reaches our returns centre. In any case we refund within 14 days of receipt.
- **Method:** to your original payment method. Amounts paid by gift card go back to the gift card. For "Pay in 3" orders, see [Payments and Billing](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/payments).
- **Amount:** the price you actually paid, after any discount. The standard delivery charge is refunded only when the whole order is returned. Any cost above standard delivery (for example the Express upgrade) is not refunded.
- **Partial returns:** delivery charges are not refunded.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different size, colour or model, return the original item and place a new order. We cannot reserve stock while a return is in progress.

## Exceptional requests

In exceptional circumstances we may extend a return window or accept an item outside these rules at our discretion. Our customer-facing teams cannot promise this in advance; every request is reviewed by the Returns team.

## FAQ

### Q: Can I return a sale item?

Yes, within 14 days of the day after delivery, as long as it is marked "Sale". Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned for change of mind.

### Q: I opened the box. Can I still return it?

Yes, if the item is complete and in good condition. The exceptions are in-ear earbuds with a broken hygiene seal and activated software.

### Q: How long do refunds take?

Within 5 working days of us receiving and inspecting your return. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

### Q: Can I swap my item for a different colour?

Not directly. Return the original item and place a new order.

### Q: My parcel was delivered on 1 October. What is my last day to return it?

For a standard item, 31 October. For a Sale item, 15 October.
