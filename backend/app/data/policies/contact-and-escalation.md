# Contact, Escalation and Complaints

## Opening hours and channels

- **Phone:** 020 7946 0321. Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:30 and Saturday 10:00 to 16:00 (UK time). Closed on Sundays and bank holidays.
- **Email:** support@lumenandoak.example.
- **Virtual assistant:** available 24 hours a day.

## Response times

| Channel or request | Target |
|---|---|
| Email | Within 1 working day (2 working days in the peak period, 20 November to 24 December) |
| Phone | Answered within about 5 minutes during opening hours |
| Safety reports | Within the hour during opening hours |
| Refund approvals | Within 1 working day of the Returns team inspecting the item |
| Complaints | Acknowledged within 2 working days |

## What our virtual assistant can and cannot do

The virtual assistant can:

- explain our policies;
- look up the status of an order for a verified customer;
- open a support request on your behalf.

It cannot:

- issue or approve refunds;
- approve exceptions to our policies;
- change payment details or account email addresses;
- action data requests;
- discuss anyone else's orders.

You can ask for a person at any time.

## When a person always handles it

A member of staff handles the following:

- any refund (refunds are always approved by a person);
- suspected fraud;
- complaints and requests to speak to a manager;
- data protection requests;
- legal threats;
- disputes over items worth more than £1,000;
- safety concerns;
- any request where you ask for a person.

## Safety concerns

If a product overheats, smokes, swells, sparks or causes injury:

1. Stop using it and unplug it.
2. Move it away from anything flammable. Do not charge it again.
3. If there is immediate danger, call 999.
4. Contact us on 020 7946 0321 or at support@lumenandoak.example and tell us it is a safety report. We arrange collection free of charge.

## Complaints

1. Tell us what went wrong and we will acknowledge it within 2 working days.
2. If you are not satisfied, ask for it to be reviewed by a senior team member.
3. We aim to give a final response within 8 weeks. If we cannot resolve it in that time, or we reach deadlock, you can refer the complaint to an approved alternative dispute resolution (ADR) body.

## Exceptional requests

Where a request falls outside our written policies (for example a late return, or a disputed return condition), our Returns team may decide in its reasonable discretion. Staff and the virtual assistant cannot promise the outcome in advance.
