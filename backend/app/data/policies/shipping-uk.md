# UK Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

- **Standard delivery:** 2 to 4 working days. Free on orders of £50 or more to mainland Great Britain, for items weighing up to 10kg (see charges below).
- **Express delivery:** next working day when you order before 14:00 on a working day. Mainland Great Britain only, for items weighing up to 10kg. Costs £6.95.

Orders placed after 14:00, or at weekends and on bank holidays, are dispatched the next working day.

## Delivery zones

- **Zone 1:** mainland Great Britain (England, Wales and Scotland excluding the Highlands and Islands).
- **Zone 2:** Northern Ireland and the Scottish Highlands and Islands. Delivery takes 1 to 2 working days longer and Express is not available. Your postcode is checked at checkout.
- **Not covered here:** the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man are handled as international destinations. See [International Shipping](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/shipping-international). We do not deliver to BFPO addresses.

## Delivery charges

Charges are based on the weight of each parcel as shown on the product page. Large items carry a "Large item" label.

| Parcel weight | Zone 1 | Zone 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 10kg, order under £50 | £3.95 | £7.95 |
| Up to 10kg, order £50 or more | Free | £4.00 |
| 10kg to 25kg | £12.95 | £16.95 |
| Over 25kg | £24.95 | £28.95 |

The "free over £50" offer applies only to parcels up to 10kg. Heavier items are always charged by weight, and Express is not available for them. A large order may ship in several parcels at no extra charge.

## Peak period

From 20 November to 24 December 2026, allow up to 2 additional working days on standard delivery estimates. Express is still next working day after dispatch, but dispatch can take up to 1 extra working day.

## Tracking

We email a tracking link when your order is dispatched. Tracking updates can lag by up to 24 hours, and a parcel may be scanned as "delivered" slightly before it arrives.

## Signature and missed deliveries

Orders over £500 need a signature. If nobody is in, the carrier will try again, or leave a card and hold the parcel at their depot for 7 days. If it is not picked up in that time, it is returned to us and we refund the order less the original delivery charge.

## Late, lost or damaged parcels

- **Late:** if your parcel has not arrived 5 working days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact us. We open a carrier trace, which can take up to 5 working days. If the parcel is not found, you can choose a replacement or a full refund.
- **Marked delivered but not received:** check safe places and with neighbours, wait 24 hours, then contact us within 14 days of the delivery scan. For orders over £500 we investigate immediately, because a signature should exist.
- **Damaged in transit:** report within 7 days with photos of the packaging and the item. This is treated as a faulty-goods claim, with a free return or replacement. See [Warranty and Faulty Goods](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/warranty).

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the address only before your order reaches the Picking stage. See [Order Cancellations and Changes](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/cancellations).

## FAQ

### Q: Is delivery free over £50?

Standard delivery is free on orders of £50 or more to mainland Great Britain, for parcels up to 10kg. Heavier parcels and Zone 2 addresses are still charged.

### Q: Can I get a large monitor delivered next day?

No. Express is only for items up to 10kg. A large monitor is sent by standard delivery for the weight-based charge.

### Q: Do you deliver to Jersey or the Isle of Man?

They are treated as international destinations, with international rates and possible local charges.

### Q: My tracking says delivered but I have nothing. What now?

Check around your property and with neighbours, wait 24 hours, then contact us within 14 days of the delivery date.
