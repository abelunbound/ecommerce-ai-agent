# Order Cancellations and Changes

## Order stages

| Stage | What it means | Can I cancel? | Can I change the address? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placed | Order received, payment authorised | Yes, free, instantly in your account | Yes |
| Processing | Being checked and allocated | Yes, free, from your account or by contacting us | Yes |
| Picking | Items being collected in our warehouse | Best effort. Phone us urgently; we cannot guarantee it | Best effort |
| Dispatched | Handed to the carrier | No. Refuse delivery or use the returns process | No |
| Delivered | Received | No. Use the returns process | No |

## Cancelling before dispatch

If your order has not been dispatched you can cancel for free. Cancelling after dispatch is not possible, but you can return the items for a refund under the [Returns and Refunds Policy](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/returns).

### Refund timing for cancellations

We release your payment within 1 working day. Your card issuer may take a further 3 to 5 working days to show it. If we had only authorised your card and not yet taken payment, the pending authorisation drops off by itself. See [Payments and Billing](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/payments).

## Personalised items

Engraved or custom-configured items can be cancelled only within 1 hour of ordering. After that, production begins and the item cannot be cancelled.

## Changing your order

We cannot edit the items in an order. To change items, cancel the order and place a new one. Prices and availability may differ.

## Changing the delivery address

Address changes are possible only before the Picking stage. Once an order is in Picking or later, we cannot change the address, and the carrier cannot redirect parcels.

## Items that go out of stock

If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we tell you within 2 working days. Available items can be dispatched in a separate parcel at no extra delivery charge. We refund unavailable items within 3 working days.

## Orders held for review

To prevent fraud we may hold an order for up to 24 hours and ask you to verify your identity. If we cannot verify you within 48 hours, we cancel the order and refund it in full.

## Pre-orders

Pre-orders can be cancelled for free at any time before dispatch.
