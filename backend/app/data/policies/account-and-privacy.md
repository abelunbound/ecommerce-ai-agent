# Account Help and Privacy

## Your account

You can check out as a guest or create an account. An account lets you track orders, register returns and download invoices.

### Password reset

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes.

### Two-step verification

Optional two-step verification is available using an authenticator app or SMS.

### Changing your email address

Change it in your account settings. You will need your password and a verification code sent to your current email address. Support staff cannot change an email address on your behalf.

### What we will never ask for

We will never ask for your password, your full card number or a one-time code in chat, email or by phone.

## Identity checks

Before we discuss an order, we ask for your order number and either the email address on the order or the delivery postcode. We cannot share order details with anyone who cannot verify.

## Your data rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **Access your data (subject access request):** free of charge. We respond within one month. For complex or numerous requests we may extend this by up to two further months, and we will tell you if so. We may ask you to verify your identity.
- **Have your data corrected.**
- **Ask us to delete your data:** we action verified account deletion requests within 30 days. We must keep some records, such as orders, invoices and tax records, for 6 years, so these are not deleted.
- **Object to or restrict processing,** including opting out of marketing at any time.
- **Complain** to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

### How to make a request

Data requests must be sent through your account settings under Privacy, or to privacy@lumenandoak.example. Our support chat cannot action data requests, but it can point you to the right place.

## Who we share data with

We share only what is needed with payment providers, delivery carriers, fraud-screening services and our customer support tools.

## Our virtual assistant

Our virtual assistant is an automated system. Conversations with it are stored for 12 months to improve our service. We remove card numbers and passwords from stored transcripts, but please do not type them into the chat. You can ask for a person at any time.
