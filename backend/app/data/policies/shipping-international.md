# International Shipping

## Where we ship

- **EU:** all EU member states.
- **US and Canada.**
- **Rest of World:** selected countries, listed at checkout.
- **Crown Dependencies:** the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man are treated as Rest of World, and local charges may apply.

We do not ship to sanctioned destinations.

## Delivery times

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

| Destination | Standard delivery |
|---|---|
| EU | 5 to 8 working days |
| US and Canada | 7 to 12 working days |
| Rest of World | 8 to 15 working days |

From 20 November to 24 December 2026, allow up to 3 additional working days.

## Delivery charges

| Parcel weight | EU | US and Canada | Rest of World |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 2kg | £9.95 | £14.95 | £19.95 |
| 2kg to 10kg | £16.95 | £24.95 | £34.95 |
| Over 10kg | £39.95 | Not available | Not available |

Free standard delivery on orders of £150 or more applies to the EU only, for parcels up to 10kg. The UK "free over £50" offer does not apply to international orders.

## Items we cannot ship internationally

Power banks and other loose lithium batteries are UK-only. Chargers are supplied with UK plugs, and no adaptor is included.

## Taxes, duties and customs

- **EU:** VAT at the destination country's rate is charged at checkout. Customs duty on consignments valued over €150 may be payable by the recipient on delivery.
- **US, Canada and Rest of World:** import duties and taxes are payable by the recipient on delivery. We do not control the amounts.

If a recipient refuses a delivery or does not pay the charges due, the parcel is returned to us. We refund the goods, less the outbound and return shipping costs.

## Returns from outside the UK

The 30-day window in the [Returns and Refunds Policy](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/returns) applies, but for change of mind you pay the return shipping. For faulty items we pay it. We refund the goods and standard delivery, but not duties or import taxes collected at the border. You may be able to reclaim these from customs.

## Tracking and delivery

We email tracking when your order is dispatched. Couriers may contact you to arrange delivery or collect duties.
