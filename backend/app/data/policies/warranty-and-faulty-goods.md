# Warranty and Faulty Goods

## Your legal rights

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. For UK customers:

- **First 30 days:** you can reject a faulty item and get a full refund.
- **After 30 days:** you are entitled to a repair or replacement. If that fails or is not possible, you can ask for a price reduction or the final right to reject, which may include a deduction for use.
- **First 6 months:** a fault is presumed to have been there at delivery unless we can prove otherwise.
- **After 6 months:** you may need to show the fault was present at delivery.
- **Time limit:** claims can be made for up to 6 years (5 years in Scotland).

Customers outside the UK may have rights under local consumer law. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights.

## The Lumen & Oak Guarantee

This is in addition to your statutory rights.

- **Lumen & Oak own-brand products:** 24 months from delivery.
- **Third-party brands:** 12 months, through the manufacturer's warranty. We handle the first-line claim for you.

### Batteries

Batteries wear with use. On own-brand products we cover a battery whose capacity falls below 80% of its rated capacity within 12 months of delivery.

### What is not covered

- Accidental damage or liquid damage.
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than us or an approved repairer.
- Normal wear and tear. Cosmetic damage consistent with normal use may not be covered, and we assess this in our discretion.

## Which route applies?

| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| Faulty, within 30 days of delivery | Full refund or replacement, free return, no deduction |
| Not faulty, within 30 days | Change-of-mind return under the [Returns and Refunds Policy](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/returns) |
| Faulty, 31 days to 6 months | Repair or replacement first |
| Faulty, after 6 months | Repair or replacement; we may ask for evidence the fault was present at delivery |

If an item is faulty within the first 30 days, you can use the faulty route rather than the change-of-mind route. We pay the return shipping, refund in full including delivery, and make no deduction.

## Dead on arrival

If an item does not work when first used within 14 days of delivery, send photos or a short video of the fault. Once we confirm the fault we dispatch a replacement without waiting for the faulty item to come back. You then return the faulty item within 14 days using the free label.

## How to make a claim

1. Contact us with your order number and a description of the fault.
2. Send photos or video, and any error messages.
3. We may ask you to run basic diagnostics.
4. We send a prepaid return label.

Repairs and replacements normally take 5 to 10 working days from when we receive the item.

## Your data

Back up and remove your data before sending any device. We are not responsible for data on returned devices.

## Safety concerns

If a product overheats, smokes, swells or sparks, stop using it and follow the safety steps in [Contact and Escalation](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/contact-and-escalation).
