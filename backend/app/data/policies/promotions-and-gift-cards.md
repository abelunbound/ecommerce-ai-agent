# Promotions, Promo Codes and Gift Cards

## Promo code rules

- One code per order. Codes cannot be combined.
- Codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed.
- Codes exclude Sale items, Final Sale items and gift cards unless the promotion says otherwise.
- Each code has an expiry date and may have a minimum spend.

### WELCOME10

10% off your first order, with a minimum spend of £30. One use per customer. It excludes Sale items, Final Sale items, gift cards and laptops.

## Threshold promotions and free gifts

Some promotions apply only above a minimum spend, such as "£20 off orders over £200" or "free £15 cable organiser kit when you spend £200 or more" (running until 30 November 2026).

If a return brings your order below the qualifying spend:

- a threshold discount is recalculated, so your refund is reduced by the part of the discount that no longer applies;
- a free gift must be returned, or its value (£15 for the cable organiser kit) is deducted from your refund.

See the [Returns and Refunds Policy](https://lumenandoak.example/policies/returns).

## Price protection

If the price of an item you bought on lumenandoak.example drops within 14 days of your order date, contact us and we refund the difference to your original payment method.

Price protection does not apply to:

- Final Sale items;
- bundles;
- orders where a promo code was used;
- price drops during the Black Friday event (20 to 30 November 2026).

## Gift cards

- Gift cards are sent by email as e-gift cards. We do not offer physical gift cards.
- Any amount from £10 to £500.
- Valid for 24 months from purchase.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- Gift cards are non-refundable, except where the law requires it.
- Gift cards are a payment method, not a discount, so they can be used together with a promo code.

### Lost or unredeemed gift cards

We can resend a gift card code only if the purchaser provides the order number and email address and the code has not been redeemed. Redeemed codes cannot be reissued.

### Refunds to gift cards

When an order paid by gift card is refunded, the amount goes back to the gift card. If the order was part paid by gift card and part by card, the refund is split in proportion. Returning money to a gift card does not extend its expiry date. If the gift card has already expired, we issue new credit valid for 12 months.

### Gift card scams

We will never ask you to pay for an order, a refund or a fee using gift cards bought elsewhere. If anyone asks you to, it is a scam.
