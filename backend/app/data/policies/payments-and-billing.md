# Payments and Billing

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Lumen & Oak gift cards, and "Pay in 3" instalments.

We do not accept bank transfer, cheque, cash or cryptocurrency.

All orders are charged in GBP. Prices shown in other currencies are estimates, and your bank may add currency conversion fees.

## When you are charged

We authorise your card when you place your order and take payment when your order is dispatched. An authorisation reserves the money on your account, and it may show as "pending" in your banking app. If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the pending authorisation drops off by itself, usually within 7 working days depending on your bank.

## "Pay in 3"

"Pay in 3" is provided by a third-party lender, Splitly.

- Three equal instalments: today, then one month and two months later.
- For orders between £60 and £1,500, for UK residents aged 18 or over, subject to approval.
- Late fees are set by the provider under its own terms; we do not set them.
- If you return an item, we refund the provider, who adjusts your remaining instalments and refunds anything you have overpaid. This can take 5 to 7 working days after we approve the refund.

## Failed payments

A payment can fail if:

- the card details or billing address do not match your bank's records;
- 3D Secure verification was not completed;
- your card limit or balance was exceeded;
- your bank flagged the transaction.

An order is created only after payment is authorised. Try again, use another method, or contact your bank.

## Charged, but no confirmation email

Wait 30 minutes and check your account's order history. Please do not place the order again until you have checked, as that can create a duplicate authorisation. If there is no order after 30 minutes, any pending authorisation will drop off, usually within 7 working days.

## Invoices and VAT

VAT invoices are available in your account under Orders, then Invoice. If you are buying for a business, enter your VAT number at checkout. We cannot add a VAT number to an order after it has been placed.

## Suspected fraud

We may cancel orders that fail our fraud checks. If we do, we refund you in full.

## FAQ

### Q: I can see two charges for one order. Why?

One may be a pending authorisation that will drop off within about 7 working days. If both have posted as completed charges, contact us.

### Q: Can I pay by bank transfer?

No.

### Q: Can I add my company's VAT number after ordering?

No. It must be entered at checkout.

### Q: Will my card be charged immediately?

We authorise at order time and take the payment when your order is dispatched.
